1) $\begin{vmatrix} a & x & \cdots & x \\ x & a & \cdots & x \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ x & x & \cdots & a \end{vmatrix}$

2) $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\ 1 & 0 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 & \cdots & 1 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ 1 & 1 & 1 & \cdots & 0 \end{vmatrix}$

3) $\begin{vmatrix} 1 & 2 & 3 & \cdots & n \\ 1 & 3 & 3 & \cdots & n \\ 1 & 2 & 4 & \cdots & n \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ 1 & 2 & 3 & \cdots & n+1 \end{vmatrix}$
